# Molaplan room pictures (assets/rooms)

Stylised flat illustrations drawn by `tool/rooms/gen_rooms.py` from simple geometric shapes
(rectangles, polygons, ellipses, lines). No photographs, stock images, fonts or third-party
artwork are used.

Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication) – own work of the Molaplan project.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

12 rooms × light / dark variants, 960×540 WebP (q 62) + 240×135 picker thumbnails.
Regenerate with `python3 tool/rooms/gen_rooms.py` (Pillow with WebP support).
